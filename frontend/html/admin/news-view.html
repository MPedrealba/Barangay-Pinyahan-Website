TITLE: Fire Hits DPWH Office in Barangay Pinyahan

DATE PUBLISHED: October 22, 2025

DESCRIPTION:

A massive fire broke out at the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) Bureau of Research and Standards building along NIA Road in Barangay Pinyahan, Quezon City, on October 22, 2025. The blaze quickly reached the third alarm, requiring more than 25 fire trucks to bring it under control. While the fire caused significant damage to the building and destroyed various pieces of research equipment, authorities reported no casualties or injuries.
