Health Services

Guidelines for Medical Assistance and Programs.

Requirements

  • Barangay Health Record / ID Card
  • PhilHealth ID (if available)
  • Vaccination Card (for infants)
  • Previous Medical Prescriptions
  • Proof of Address

Procedure

  1. 1 Sign-in at the Health Center Desk
  2. 2 Initial check-up (Vitals/Weight)
  3. 3 Consultation with Doctor/Midwife
  4. 4 Laboratory or Medicine dispensing
  5. 5 Schedule follow-up appointment
