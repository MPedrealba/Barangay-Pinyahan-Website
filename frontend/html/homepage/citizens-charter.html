CITIZEN'S CHARTER

Mandate: Republic Act No. 11032 or the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018. This Citizen's Charter details the frontline services available to our citizens, the step-by-step procedures, requirements, processing times, and the designated personnel responsible for each action. We are committed to transparency, efficiency, and excellent public service delivery.

Issuance of Barangay Clearance

Client Steps Agency Action Fees Time Responsible
1. Secure application form and present requirements:
  • Valid Government-issued ID
  • Proof of Residency
  • Cedula
 Receive and verify the submitted documents. Provide the application form if not yet filled. None 5 mins Barangay Secretary / Receiving Clerk
2. Pay the corresponding clearance fee. Accept payment and issue an Official Receipt (OR). ₱ 50.00 3 mins Barangay Treasurer
3. Present Official Receipt and wait. Encode details, print the Clearance, and route for Punong Barangay's signature. None 10 mins Admin Staff / Punong Barangay
4. Receive the signed Barangay Clearance. Log the transaction and release the document to the citizen. None 2 mins Releasing Clerk

Issuance of Certificate of Indigency

Client Steps Agency Action Fees Time Responsible
1. Approach the desk, state purpose, and present Valid ID / Proof of Residency. Verify residency and socio-economic status records of the requesting individual. None 5 mins Barangay Secretary / Social Worker
2. Wait for the processing of the document. Type the Certificate of Indigency and have it signed by the Punong Barangay. None 10 mins Admin Staff / Punong Barangay
3. Sign the logbook and receive the certificate. Release the signed Certificate of Indigency. None 2 mins Releasing Clerk
