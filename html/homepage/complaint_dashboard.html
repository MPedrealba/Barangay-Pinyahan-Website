CITIZEN'S CHARTER

FRONTLINE SERVICES FEEDBACK & COMPLAINTS

I. Frontline Services

  • Issuance of Barangay Clearance
  • Issuance of Barangay Business Permit / Clearance
  • Issuance of Certificate of Indigency
  • Issuance of Certificate of Residency
  • Issuance of Barangay ID
  • Filing of Complaints (Katarungang Pambarangay)
  • Request for Barangay Protection Order
  • Request for Assistance (Medical, Burial, etc.)

II. Vision

  • A progressive, peaceful, and well-governed barangay that promotes the welfare of its constituents through efficient and transparent public service.

III. Mission

  • To deliver accessible, prompt, and quality frontline services to the residents of Barangay Pinyahan.
  • To uphold transparency and accountability in all barangay transactions.
  • To continuously improve service delivery and responsiveness to community needs.

IV. Service Pledge

  • We commit to serve every resident with courtesy, integrity, and efficiency.
  • We shall process all requests within the prescribed period and inform the public of any delays.
  • We welcome feedback and complaints to improve our services.

V. Feedback & Complaints Mechanism

  • Feedback forms are available at the Barangay Hall reception desk.
  • You may also submit feedback via email at brgypinyahan@gmail.com.
  • For complaints, please visit the Barangay Hall or call (02) 1234-5678.
  • All complaints will be acknowledged within 3 working days.
